# V2.2 R2 Pointer Reveal Validation

## Expected
- Pointer in Hero Stage selects one of three same-Anchor Moment reframings.
- Reveal opens at the opposite/side empty zone, not on the main face.
- Portal hover/tap activates the same source Anchor variation.
- No face replacement, morph, acrylic, black matte, polygon or skin material overlay.

## Desktop zones
- left third → Stage Light
- center third → Quiet Detail
- right third → Close Moment
- reveal side is deliberately moved away from the pointer/face center

## Mobile
- Portal tap opens the same-Anchor reveal.
- Horizontal swipe changes H1~H6.
- Pointer-only behavior is not required.
