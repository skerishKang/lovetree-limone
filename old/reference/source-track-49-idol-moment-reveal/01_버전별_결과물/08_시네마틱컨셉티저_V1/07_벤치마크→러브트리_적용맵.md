## LoveTree-specific cinematic grammar
1. **Moment:** an image becomes important because the viewer emotionally stops there.
2. **Save:** a warm moving light marks that Moment instead of showing a software form.
3. **Connection:** the same light travels/match-cuts into the next Moment.
4. **Memory Field:** saved moments gain depth and the camera physically passes between them.
5. **Tree:** light paths converge into roots/branches, then bloom into a large luminous tree with actual Moment imagery hanging on it.

This is consistent with LoveTree’s product constitution: the basic unit is the Moment, and Connection is the emotional cause that leads to the next exploration—not just chronological order.
