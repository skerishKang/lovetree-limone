## Actual functional controls
- **SKIP:** immediately seeks to the final LoveTree brand scene.
- **REPLAY:** returns timeline to 00:00 and starts again.
- **ENTER LOVETREE:** stops teaser, opens the internal Moment experience.
- **Moment cards:** open an actual modal preview in the internal experience.
- **Tree Moment frames:** open the same real Moment modal system.
- **SOUND:** muted by default; optional locally generated WebAudio ambience, no benchmark audio.
